A letter I wrote for you

My wifey,

When I read your monthsary message, I kept thinking about one sentence: “here I am still choosing you.” I want you to know that sentence landed somewhere deep in me.

Five months isn't forever yet. But it is five months of choosing, learning, missing, laughing, overthinking, reassuring, growing and making memories. And I don't want perfect love. I want the kind where we keep coming back to each other and keep becoming better for each other.

You said you imagine our future. I do too. I imagine the day distance isn't a screen anymore. I imagine holding your hand, annoying you in person, stealing your food, making you laugh when you don't want to, and finally getting to say “I love you” without a call ending between us.

And your birthday is coming. Your 20th. So yes, this month is going to be busy for me — but somewhere inside all the chaos, there will always be a little space that belongs to you.

You don't have to know every version of my past. You only need to know that I want to be intentional about the version of me you meet in our future.

Thank you for choosing me. Thank you for loving me even when I'm not the easiest person to love. And thank you for giving me another month to prove that my words aren't just words.

There is one more thing I want to ask you.

But you have to reach the end. 💜

The final question

Will you keep choosing me?

Not just for the pretty days. For the weird days, busy days, quiet days, distance days, laughing-until-we-can't-breathe days — all of them.

My beautiful wifey,

If you clicked YES, then I have only one thing left to say:

I choose you.

I choose you when we're laughing. I choose you when we're confused. I choose you when distance makes everything harder. I choose you when I have to learn how to love you better. I choose you because somewhere along the way, “you and me” stopped sounding like an idea and started feeling like home.

I don't know every detail of our future yet. But I know the person I want beside me while we discover it.

So here's my little monthsary promise: I will keep putting in the effort. I will keep learning you. I will keep making room for you. And when life gets loud, I will remember that the girl behind this screen is someone worth choosing again and again.

Five months down. So many more memories waiting.

Happy 5th Monthsary, my love. 💜
Forever your future husband,
Jonathan
